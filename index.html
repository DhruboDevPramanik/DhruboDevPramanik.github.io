About Me

Hello! I am Dhrubo Dev Pramanik.

I'm a final-year Computer Science & Engineering student at RUET, currently finishing my undergraduate thesis on communication-efficient federated learning for brain tumor segmentation. My research interests sit at the intersection of Federated Learning, Deep Learning, Image Segmentation, and Compression & Quantization. Alongside research, I build practical software — from Flask-based ML web apps to backend work in PHP/Laravel — and I enjoy taking a project from a rough idea to something people can actually use.
